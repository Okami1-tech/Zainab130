A MESSAGE FROM MY HEART

For My Sarauniya

One feeling, spoken across the world. 💗

Every language has a place in my heart.

My Sarauniya 💗

Take a moment and look at that heart again. Every little expression you just watched appear represents the same beautiful message, written in a different language.

French, Italian, Spanish, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, Hausa, Yoruba, Swahili, and so many more. Different languages, different alphabets, different corners of the world, all brought together in one place.

There are dozens of expressions of love in that little heart. Some you can read, some you may need to translate, and some might be completely unfamiliar to you. But they all carry one message.

Three simple words: I LOVE YOU.

I wanted to do something different. I didn't want to just send you another ordinary message saying how special you are. I wanted to create something that would make you stop for a moment, look at it, and realise how much thought went into every little detail.

Imagine searching through languages from different parts of the world, gathering their beautiful ways of expressing love, and bringing them together to form a single heart. A heart that has no language barrier, no borders, and no distinction between cultures.

And yet, after gathering all those words, I still feel like something is missing.

Because even if I could find every language spoken on this earth, learn every expression, discover every beautiful word for love, and fill every corner of that heart with them, I would still struggle to find words that completely explain what you mean to me.

Isn't that something? So many languages. So many ways to say it. And still, not enough words.

That's why I didn't want this to be just a heart made of text. I wanted it to represent something bigger: that some feelings are simply too beautiful to be confined to one language.

My Sarauniya, you are someone I thank Allah for. Someone whose happiness matters to me. Someone I remember in my duas, and someone I pray Allah blesses with happiness, protection, success and everything good in this life.

I pray Allah protects your heart, makes your difficult days easier, grants the wishes you keep quietly in your heart, and fills your life with peace, mercy and barakah.

And if Allah has written a beautiful future for us together, may He make the journey easy, keep our intentions pure, and bring everything together in the most beautiful halal way.

One day, you might forget the exact words written in this heart. You might forget how many languages were there or which one appeared first.

But I hope you remember the thought behind it. Someone took expressions from across the world and brought them together just to give you something different.

And if there is one thing I want you to take away from this little creation, it is this:

Thousands of words.
Dozens of languages.
Countless ways to express one feeling.

And still, my message remains the same.

I LOVE YOU, MY SARAUNIYA. 💗

With all my love,
Muhammad 💗
